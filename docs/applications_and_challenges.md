# Applications and challenges

Source: Sections I–IV of the original report.

## Applications

- Remote and rural broadband coverage
- Disaster response and emergency messaging
- Precision agriculture and infrastructure monitoring
- Remote IoT connectivity
- Satellite backhaul and direct-to-cellular services

## Engineering challenges

| Challenge | Design concern |
|---|---|
| Propagation delay | Timing, retransmission and real-time services |
| Doppler and mobility | Frequency synchronization and handover |
| SWaP-C | Size, weight, power and cost of payload hardware |
| Space environment | Radiation, temperature and equipment accessibility |
| Spectrum | Coordination and interference management |
| Security | Protection of distributed infrastructure and communications |

NTN complements terrestrial networks; coverage, data rate and service continuity depend on the deployment and terminal capabilities. The report does not establish guaranteed worldwide service or validated ultra-low-latency performance.
